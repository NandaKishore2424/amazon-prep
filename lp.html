Why this page is longer than the DSA one

In the interview experiences collected on this site, the Leadership Principles are what decided outcomes — not the code.

  • An SDE-II in Bangalore was rejected after clean technical performance in every round. HR cited an average rating on Customer Obsession as the deciding factor. Nothing technical went wrong.
  • The Feb 2026 SDE-1 who was selected called the LP round the toughest of the day — roughly 40 minutes of a poker-faced interviewer going deep into each situation, asking multiple follow-ups and challenging decisions from different angles.
  • The L5 who got an offer in Bangalore spent 45 minutes on LP with the hiring manager and 50 more with the Bar Raiser. Two of four rounds were almost entirely behavioural.
  • Meanwhile candidates who passed 8 of 13 test cases, or never reached the optimal DP solution, were still selected.

The asymmetry is the point. Imperfect code with sound reasoning survives. An average LP rating does not.
